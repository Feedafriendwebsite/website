Food Boxes

Need a Helping Hand?

A box filled with love, hope & sustenance.
At Feed a Friend, we aim to support those most in need with essential food items. Each box is filled with essential grocery items to help carry you through the month with dignity and care. Boxes are delivered once a month.

Who can apply?

1
Be a group of 2 or more people (we do not provide food for individuals)
AND
You must meet at least one of the following:
2
Currently out of work
3
Have not received a salary for at least 2 months
4
Be a family with children, with a total income of less than 1500 QAR after rent is paid only

Important: Submitting an application does not guarantee a food pack. We review each application carefully and do our best to assist based on need and availability.
