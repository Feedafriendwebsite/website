Host a community fridge.

For every fridge

What every fridge needs.

These apply to both routes. The host provides the land the fridge stands on.

Open to everyone

Accessible to all, regardless of gender or religion. So it goes outside, on land the public can reach.

Shelter from the sun

A roof or canopy over the fridge to keep it out of the Qatar sun.

Raised platform

The fridge sits on a raised platform, off the ground.

Outdoor plug socket

A weatherproof socket close by, so the fridge runs all day.

Mosques: inside a mosque isn't suitable, because a community fridge has to be open to everyone. Just outside the mosque, in an accessible spot, works well.

Two routes

Pick the one that's you.

Route 1 · Private landowners

I own my home

If you own your home, you can install a fridge outside it, on your own land. It's your personal charity, your Sadaqa.

Your own fridgeYou provide the fridge.
No permission neededIt's on private land.
  1. Choose your spotOutside your home, on your land, where the public can reach it.
  2. Prepare the siteShelter from the sun, a raised platform, and an outdoor plug socket.
  3. Install your fridgePlug it in and open it to the community.
  4. Tell us about itLet us know so we can add it to the fridge map for others to find.
Renting? The land has to be yours to offer, so this route is for homeowners.

Route 2 · Schools

I'm a school

A school can install a fridge outside, in its grounds. We provide the fridge, once you have permission from your municipality.

We provide the fridgeA double-door Feed a Friend fridge.
Permission firstFrom your local municipality.
  1. Get municipality permissionThere are forms to complete in Arabic, plus photos and plans to submit. The process differs from one municipality to another, so please ask your Arabic-speaking PRO to visit and clarify it.
  2. Contact us with your permissionOnce you have permission, reach out to us for the next steps.
  3. Prepare the siteShelter from the sun, a raised platform, and an outdoor plug socket.
  4. Set up your student teamA student leadership team takes responsibility for the fridge, with a class rota (see below).
Please don't install before you have permission. Your municipality decides, not us.

Looking after it

Run by students, every day.

We encourage a student leadership team to take responsibility for a school's fridge, with each class taking a turn to refill it daily.

Refill

The class on duty restocks the fridge.

Clean

Wipe and tidy the fridge every day.

Inspect

Check all the food, daily.

Discard

Throw away any unwanted food items.

A class rota

Each school day belongs to a different class, so the load is shared and nobody is left to do it alone.
